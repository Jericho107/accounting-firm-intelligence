# Limitations

- All clients, invoices, time entries, obligations and capacity values are synthetic.
- The SQLite warehouse demonstrates analytical modelling and reconciliation, not production-scale accounting infrastructure.
- Filing and collection thresholds are illustrative and require calibration to the jurisdiction, service line and practice policy.
- Profitability uses simplified billed-fee and labour-cost logic; overhead allocation and write-offs are not modelled.
- Capacity is expressed as available versus worked hours and does not model skills, leave, scheduling constraints or forward staffing demand.
- The generated HTML surface is a decision artefact, not a substitute for a production BI semantic model.
- No statutory audit, assurance opinion, tax advice or realised client ROI is claimed.
